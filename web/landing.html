An open firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1,
with a studio and an emulator in the browser.

Quantum GammaWave runs on the FM-1's own hardware: a GammaWave-style HUD, eight synthesis engines, a 49-genre song generator with a TAKES reel and a punch-in FX deck — plus a web editor over Web MIDI and a browser emulator you can play without the pedal.

What it isOne firmware, three ways to meet it

All of it comes from the same sources: the firmware you install is the one the emulator runs and the one the Web Studio talks to.

The firmware

For the M-VAVE FM-1 (AC79NN). Eight engines (ANALOG, PHASE, LOFI, VOICE, WHEEL, DRUM-X, NOISE, FM6), VOWEL/SCREAM filters, Hearth macros, the GEN generator with 49 genres and the TAKES reel.

The Web Studio

An editor in the browser: change the sound over Web MIDI on a real FM-1, or run it as a simulator with demo songs, four tape lanes and WAV export.

The emulator

The firmware compiled to WebAssembly: the 240×240 screen, every LED, all the controls, MIDI in and audio out — with a flash image that survives a reload.

ScreensRendered by the firmware itself

HOME, the punch-in FX keys, the FX rack and the DRUM page in the QUANTUM palette — 240×240 frames written by the firmware's own drawing code, not mock-ups. Every screen of every palette is rendered and checked on each commit.

InstallOn a real M-VAVE FM-1

  1. Check the package. Download the .fwsc and its SHA256SUMS: the installer refuses to write anything without a matching hash.
  2. Connect with a USB data cable (no hub), computer on mains power, other MIDI apps closed.
  3. Coming from another custom firmware? Return to the official V15 first: the installer offers it and validates the official file by hash and by layout.
  4. Press VERIFY, then INSTALL. Over GammaWave/JIANT a backup of your songs, sounds and settings is downloaded first, and you confirm it before anything is written.
  5. Do not unplug. If a write is cut off, reconnect and press INSTALL again: the FM-1 stays in update mode and the loader finishes the write.

Only the M-VAVE FM-1. No other model is supported, and this firmware has not been tried on a physical FM-1 yet — it is tested against a simulated device.

Integrity is not authenticity. The SHA-256 proves the bytes are the published ones, not who published them; a package signature is designed and not implemented yet.

A firmware update can brick a device. Read the recovery steps before you start: installation & recovery.

RoadmapWhat is done, what is not

Available

The full GW-1 feature set, the QUANTUM palette and identity, 141 presets, PHASER, punch-in FX, the installer with a mandatory SHA-256, the emulator and the whole host test suite.

Experimental

The punch-in FX layer and the PHASER: implemented and tested in code, not tried on a real FM-1. The emulator's audio path.

Planned

Package signing (Ed25519), a real CPU and RAM measurement on the device, shared audio buffers, preset sharing, UI polish.
